O Caminho

A redesign of the whole mobile surface — nine screens, one live prototype. Tap through it on the right.

What's wrong today

It reads as a worksheet, not a place. Every screen is the same stack of white cards on cream. There's no branded surface, no texture, no signature moment — nothing that could only be this app.

Every lesson is a dead end. You finish, you're returned to a list, and you have to decide what to do next. Duolingo never asks you to decide.

Five moves
1
Calçada, not a road

Progression is a Portuguese mosaic pavement — diamond stones on a cobble trail. Same job as Duolingo's path, but it belongs to Portugal instead of to Duolingo.

2
An azulejo band on every screen

A deep olive header with a tile pattern, carrying the streak and the daily ring. One held colour field gives the app an identity the card stack never could.

3
Fraunces at display scale

Portuguese phrases go up to 30–34px and every number — streak, XP, due count — is set in the display face. The type does the work the illustrations would have done.

4
Sessions chain — this is the flow fix

A lesson never ends on a list. It ends on the next stone, already named and timed: "A seguir: 6 palavras para rever · 2 min". The path scrolls itself. Finish the drill on the right and you'll see it.

5
Loud mechanics, quiet palette

Streak, XP, league, word of the day, SRS queue — all in. But rendered in terra and olive, never neon. Terra stays a correction colour, so nothing scolds.

Borrowed, and from where
Path — Duolingo Rapid word tiles — Drops Native clips — Memrise Dialogue + transcript — Practice Portuguese SRS queue — Anki League — Duolingo
Notes & next steps

I couldn't reach the attached port.robertjeremiah.com folder from here, so the screens are built from PORT DS — its tokens, its five real components (AudioButton, Recorder, AnswerDiff, VerbConjugator, Markdown) and the conventions in its guide. Layouts that exist in your app but not in the DS are my proposal, not a recreation.

Worth doing next: the Drops-style visual drill needs real photography — right now it's a placeholder grid. And the family view assumes each learner has their own level; tell me if it's one shared plan instead.
