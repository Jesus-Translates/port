## 3. Market — ranked segments

1. **CIPLE-driven non-Lusophone residents in Portugal (~700–780k).** The wedge.
   Exam seats sell out in <6 hours; ciple-alert.com profitably charges $3.99/mo
   for seat alerts alone (strongest revealed-preference signal found). Alternative
   is ~€28/hr Preply tutoring (≈€224/mo at recommended cadence). Frame as
   **outcome ("pass CIPLE A2")**, never category ("learn Portuguese").
2. **Civics/culture test (new Nationality Law, in force 19 May 2026) — the
   land-grab.** 810k+ CPLP residents exempt from the language test but NOT the
   civics test; no incumbent. **BUT implementing regulations still unpublished —
   build the delivery shell/question engine now, commission content only when
   the regulation publishes.** Watch Diário da República weekly.
3. **Anglophone D7/D8 households (25–40k units), in-country AND pre-move.**
   Family plan earns its keep here. Pitch vs Duolingo Super Family ($119.99/yr):
   "wrong Portuguese for where you live," never price.
4. **Heritage diaspora — rank LAST, confidently.** Camões EPE tuition went free
   2025/26; EPE certifications halved. Subsidised against us and contracting.

## 6. Operating setup — verdict

- **Portuguese Lda. Not Estonia, not Ireland.** Portugal taxes on head office OR
  effective management — a company run from Torres Vedras is Portuguese
  tax-resident regardless of where incorporated. Offshore = double compliance +
  a losing PE argument. Lda: ~16.5% effective on first €50k profit (15% SME rate
  + derrama). Estonia's 22% distribution tax is *higher*.
- **The real money is personal: IFICI (NHR 2.0)** — 20% flat vs 48% top marginal.
  Time-limited, 5-year non-residence lookback. **Confirm eligibility with a
  Portuguese tax adviser BEFORE incorporating — if arrival was >5 years ago the
  door is shut.**
- **VAT:** use the €10k cross-border threshold months 1–6 (charge PT VAT to all),
  pre-register Union OSS in Portugal before breaching it. Two location-evidence
  pieces per sale; confirm retention rules with the contabilista.
- **Sell web-first via Stripe + Stripe Tax; DMA link-out for EU iOS users.**
  Web nets ~€11.75 vs ~€10.36 via Apple IAP at 15% — worth ~€17/subscriber/yr.
  Google Play EEA fees restructured effective 30 Jun 2026 (10% new installs
  + 5% billing fee) — model explicitly. Do not launch IAP-only. Paddle only if
  we want zero VAT compliance at ~5%.

## 7. Top risks (full list in raw output)

1. **Register authenticity IS the product, and AI-generated content with no
   native review is the fastest way to destroy it.** One Brazilianism
   screenshotted into Algarve Expats falsifies the positioning at zero cost to
   the poster. Required, permanent: native pt-PT reviewer sign-off on 100% of
   canonical content; a Brazilianism linter in CI (lexicon, gerund vs estar a +
   inf., pronoun placement); AI tutor never generates canonical/assessed content.
2. **AI-tutor parity** (Hermínia) — see premise correction 1.
3. **Family-plan moat is one sprint wide** for Practice Portuguese.
4. **CIPLE thesis = bet on one government policy, and the bottleneck is exam
   SEAT supply we don't control.** Customer can study well and fail to get a
   seat — they'll blame us. Never promise outcomes; consider shipping free
   seat-availability alerts ourselves as the acquisition hook (it's the actual
   felt pain, already monetised at $3.99/mo by someone else).
5. **No official CIPLE pass-rate data exists** → launch copy must be process
   claims, not results (DECO/ASAE exposure). Instrument self-reported pass
   outcomes from day one.
6. **Unmetered AI tutor on a €14.99 flat plan is unbounded COGS** — cap/meter
   before launch. Azure Speech is a single-vendor dependency for the pt-PT voices.
7. **Reachable audience ≪ addressable market:** channels are 35k (Algarve) and
   300–600-member (Oeste) groups that punish ads. CAC must stay under ~€40 on
   €119 ARPU → community seeding, SEO, partnerships; paid social is not the engine.

## 8. Appendix — all 45 raw name candidates (4 lenses, verbatim from the panel)

### Lens: portuguese-word
- **Tasca** — *Portuguese, the way it's actually spoken.*: A tasca is the small, unpretentious neighbourhood tavern where Portuguese people actually eat, argue and talk — an institution in Portugal and a word Brazil does not use, so it silently certifies the dialect. Two syllables, spells itself after one hearing; the Spanish and Italian senses (tavern, pocket) are harmless.
- **Bica** — *One bica a day.*: The small strong espresso you order in Lisbon — Brazil says cafezinho, so the word is a hard pt-PT marker. Perfect metaphor for a short daily lesson; four letters; doubles as a tutor-persona name.
- **Farol** — *Find your bearings in Portuguese.*: Lighthouse — incl. Cabo Carvoeiro, minutes from the family's coast. Guidance, orientation, a fixed point you steer by; ready-made logo mark.
- **Ponte** — *Cross into Portuguese.*: Bridge — cleanest metaphor, but the most exhausted one in language learning.
- **Passo** — *Passo a passo.*: Step — names the lesson-path mechanic; English eye sees "pass" next to CIPLE prep.
- **Petisco** — *Small plates of Portuguese, every day.*: Small shared plates → bite-sized daily lessons + family table in one image.
- **Aldeia** — *Learn Portuguese together.*: Village — belonging, neighbours; every family account is "your aldeia".
- **Ainda** — *Ainda não. Not yet — but soon.*: Growth mindset as a real Portuguese word; best as tutor voice/in-app copy.
- **Marco** — *Mark how far you've come.*: Milestone marker; navigators' stone pillars; risk: reads as a man's first name.
- **Sotaque** — *Get the accent right.*: Accent — names the buyer's core fear (being taught Brazilian by accident) and the product's pronunciation differentiator.
- **Devagar** — *Devagar se vai ao longe.*: Anti-streak stance; wrong promise for a deadline-driven CIPLE buyer.
- **Encontro** — *Every lesson is a conversation.*: Meeting/encounter; reads pan-Romance.
